## Central question
How should longitudinal patient histories be represented and grouped so that the resulting phenotypes capture clinically relevant differences in future outcomes and disease progression?

## Organizing argument
Dynamic-DeepHit establishes dynamic individual prognosis. ODTP examines discrete temporal phenotypes through future outcomes. CAMELOT examines supervised clustering, imbalance, collapse, and feature-time interpretation for categorical outcomes. VaDeSC-EHR offers a joint generative view of longitudinal histories and survival. This is a conceptual progression, not a claim that the methods form a direct extension chain. Predictive performance alone does not establish phenotype validity.
